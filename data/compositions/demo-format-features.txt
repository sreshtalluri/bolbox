tal: tintal
lay: 100
title: Format feature demonstration
source: >
  NOT a traditional composition. Constructed to exercise every feature of the
  format so the parser has something real to chew on. Sreshta -- replace this
  with actual kaidas from your own books; this file exists to prove the format
  works, not to be played.

# 1. Plain bols, one per matra.
dha  dhin dhin dha  | dha  dhin dhin dha  | dha  tin  tin  ta   | ta   dhin dhin dha

# 2. A compound bol filling one matra. `tirakita` is four strokes in the space
#    of one `dha`. This is the case the whole project is organised around.
dha  tirakita dhin na   | dha  tirakita dhin na   | ta   tirakita tin  na   | ta   tirakita dhin dha

# 3. Explicit grouping. `{2, ti ra}` is the same duration as one bol, split.
#    Equivalent to writing a two-stroke compound, but spelled out.
dha  {2, ti ra}  dhin na   | dha  {2, ti ra}  dhin na   | ta   {2, ti ra}  tin  na   | ta   {2, ti ra}  dhin dha

# 4. Nested groups. First half of the matra is `dha`, second half is four
#    strokes -- so those four land at 1/2, 5/8, 3/4 and 7/8 of the matra.
dha  {2, dha {4, te re ki ta}}  dhin na   | dha  dhin dhin dha  | ta   tin  tin  ta   | ta   dhin dhin dha

# 5. Rests, and a seven-way group. Seven does not divide the MIDI tick grid
#    evenly, which is exactly why positions are exact fractions internally.
dha  -    dha  -    | {7, ta ki ta ta ki ta ka}  dha  dhin na   | ta   -    tin  -    | ta   dhin dhin dha

# 6. Accents. `!` is an accent, `.` is a ghost note. These override the
#    positional default rather than stacking with it.
dha! dhin dhin dha. | dha  dhin dhin dha  | ta   tin  tin  ta   | ta   dhin dhin dha!

# 7. Alternate spellings. Every bol on this line resolves through the alias
#    table: ghe->ge, ke->ka, ra->re, trkt->tirakita.
ghe  ke   na   ra   | dha  trkt dhin na   | ta   tin  tin  ta   | ta   dhin dhin dha
