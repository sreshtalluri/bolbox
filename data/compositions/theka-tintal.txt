# The basic theka. Sixteen matras, four vibhags of four.
# Sam is matra 1. The third vibhag is khali -- the bayan lifts, so `dhin`
# becomes `tin` and `dha` becomes `ta`. The parser knows the khali vibhag from
# the tal definition and drops velocity there automatically; the bol change
# here is the musical one, written out because that is how it is played.

dha  dhin dhin dha  | dha  dhin dhin dha  | dha  tin  tin  ta   | ta   dhin dhin dha
